# Product Vision

Toem Here! is a playful mobile app for discovering, photographing, naming, ranking, and collecting monitor lizards across Bangkok parks.

The product should feel local, social, and collectible. The core loop is simple:

1. A user spots a monitor lizard.
2. They photograph and submit the sighting.
3. The app identifies or creates a monitor profile.
4. Other users can view, vote, collect, and follow sightings.

## Target Users

- Bangkok park visitors.
- Wildlife-curious locals and tourists.
- Users who enjoy collection, ranking, and lightweight social discovery.
- Community members who want a fun map of monitor lizard activity.

## Product Pillars

- Discovery: make sightings easy to find on a live map.
- Collection: let users build a personal monitor lizard collection.
- Personality: make each monitor profile memorable with names, traits, and rarity.
- Community: support voting, ranking, sharing, and recent activity.
- Trust: prevent low-quality submissions and protect user/location data.

## MVP Success

The MVP is successful when a user can:

- Open the app and understand the purpose quickly.
- See monitor lizards ranked by popularity.
- View monitor details.
- Browse sightings by park or map.
- Submit a sighting with a photo and location.
- Vote for a monitor.
- See a basic profile/collection summary.
