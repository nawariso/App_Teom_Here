# Future Feature Ideas

These ideas are intentionally parked for later so the MVP can stay focused.

## Bangkok Park Passport

Users collect visual stamps for each supported park. Stamps can unlock after a visit, sighting, or completed micro-quest.

## Park Micro-Quests

Small park-specific tasks such as spotting a lakeside area, visiting before sunset, or recording a safe wildlife observation.

## Field Guide And Safety Tips

A concise guide for safe distance, no feeding, no harassment, and respectful wildlife photography.

## Confirm Or Dismiss Sightings

Users can mark a sighting as still visible or no longer visible. This can improve confidence and ranking quality.

## Park Pocket Guide

Each park gets a practical detail page with entrances, transit tips, facilities, accessibility notes, shaded routes, and recent sightings.

## Collectible Stickers

Users earn Bangkok-inspired stickers such as monitor lizards, lotus, rain trees, canal boats, and park icons.

## Watch Zones

Users save parks or areas they care about and see nearby sightings first.
